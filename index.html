How this is worked out
Purchases
Each purchase your phone sends after an Apple Pay payment, tap or online, saved in your private spending repository on GitHub.
Duplicates
A tap email and a Wallet notification for the same amount within ten minutes count once.
Categories
Matched from the merchant name first. If that isn't recognised, the category your phone picked is used. Anything else is Other.
Refunds
Counted as negative spending, so they lower the totals.
Left out
Declined payments, test emails with no amount, and anything not paid with Apple Pay.
Settings

The token that lets this app read your private spending repository is saved only on this phone.
